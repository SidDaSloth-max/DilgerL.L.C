Website Design Built for Speed, SEO, and Conversions

A lot of small business websites fail for the same reason: they were built with a page builder loaded with plugins, tracking scripts, and bloated theme files that make the site slow before a single line of real content ever loads. Slow websites lose visitors before they read a word, and page speed is itself a factor in how Google ranks a site — so a slow website isn't just a bad experience, it's actively working against you in search results. Our website design service starts from the opposite direction: a clean, hand-built site with no more code than the page actually needs, so it loads fast on both a fiber connection and a spotty phone signal in a parking lot.

Every site we build is mobile-first, since most local searches and a large share of all web traffic now happen on a phone. That means the mobile layout isn't an afterthought squeezed out of a desktop design — it's the starting point, with the desktop version built up from there. Combined with SEO fundamentals (clean heading structure, fast load times, schema markup, proper meta tags) built in from the first line of code, a new site from Dilger L.L.C. is ready to be found on Google from the day it launches, not months later after a separate SEO cleanup project.

Simple, Maintainable Sites Over Bloated Platforms

For most small business websites, a hand-built static site loads faster and is easier to keep secure than a large content-management platform carrying plugins you don't need. There's no plugin to go out of date, no database to get hacked, and no monthly platform fee beyond hosting and a domain. If your business eventually needs something a static site can't do — a customer login, a booking system, e-commerce with inventory — we'll say so upfront and build (or recommend) the right tool for that instead of forcing a workaround.

Whether you need a brand-new site or your current one just needs a rebuild, the SEO work doesn't stop at launch — see our SEO services page for how we keep a site's rankings improving after it goes live, and our Google Business Profile page for the local-search piece that pairs with almost every new site we build.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a new website?

Most small business websites take 2–4 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on the number of pages, how much content needs to be written, and how quickly we get feedback and assets from you.

Do you write the content, or do I need to provide it?

We write SEO-focused content for every page as part of the build. We'll ask you for the real, specific details about your business (what you actually do, your service area, honest trust signals) so the content is accurate, not generic filler.

Will my site work well on mobile phones?

Yes — every site is designed mobile-first and tested across phone, tablet, and desktop screen sizes before launch.

Do I own the website once it's built?

Yes, for a standard website design project the finished site is yours. (Our separate rank-and-rent service works differently — that's a leased lead-generation arrangement, not a site you own, and we'll always be clear about which model applies to your project.)

Can you redesign my existing website instead of starting over?

Often, yes. If your current site has good bones but looks dated or loads slowly, a redesign is usually faster and cheaper than a full rebuild — we'll tell you honestly which approach fits after reviewing your current site.
