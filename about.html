About Dilger L.L.C.

A U.S.-based SEO and website design company, founded in 2026 — here's our story and how we work.

Our Story

Dilger L.L.C. was founded in 2026 with a simple starting point: most small businesses don't need a bloated marketing retainer or a website stuffed with plugins — they need a fast site that actually shows up on Google, and someone straightforward to work with while it gets built. We provide SEO services, website design, Google Business Profile management, and rank-and-rent lead generation for small businesses across the United States, and we work remotely with clients nationwide rather than limiting ourselves to one metro area.

We're a small, founder-led company, and we'd rather be upfront about exactly where we are than dress it up. We don't have twenty years of case studies or a stack of five-star reviews yet — what we do have is a clear, honest process, real technical SEO knowledge, and two real client projects we can point to: a local SEO site for Alex's Cords and Bundles, and a website currently in development for Dilger Custom Cabinetry. See our portfolio for details on both.

Because we're not managing a large roster of accounts, every project gets direct, hands-on attention — the same person scoping your SEO strategy is the one implementing it, not an account manager relaying instructions to someone else.

How We Approach SEO

We build for how Google actually evaluates sites today: real content, clean technical fundamentals, honest local signals, and a genuinely good experience for the person reading the page — not keyword-stuffed text or manipulative link schemes that put a site at risk of a penalty. That also means we won't fabricate reviews, invent years of experience we don't have, or claim licenses and credentials that aren't real. If a claim can't be backed up, it doesn't go on the page — yours or ours.
